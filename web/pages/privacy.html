Private by design

Your workspace stays yours.

The current beta stores workspace data in extension-local storage in your browser profile. It does not include an account, telemetry, or cloud upload.

What is stored

Workspace names, descriptions, URLs, tab titles, groups, collections, preferences, and local history. Opt-in daily capture runs locally at your chosen time and is off by default.

What is not copied

ROVER does not copy cookies, passwords, sign-ins, form contents, private browsing sessions, or browser history.

Your controls

Download a Full local backup from settings, export workspaces, delete local records, or uninstall the extension after backing up. Review exported URLs and titles before sharing them.

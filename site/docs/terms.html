ROVER Workspaces trial and subscription terms

Pre-release commercial terms; no active purchase offer.

On this page

Last updated: October 3, 2026. These are pre-release disclosures for a product in development. They do not create a subscription, payment obligation, or promise of availability.

Current status

ROVER Workspaces does not currently offer a paid checkout, recurring billing, or store purchase. The local extension trial is provided for product testing only. Do not send payment details through GitHub issues or support messages.

Planned subscription behavior

If a paid plan launches, it is expected to renew at the interval selected during checkout until cancelled. The checkout page will show the price, currency, taxes, renewal date, merchant of record, and cancellation instructions before confirmation. A purchaser account, rather than a browser profile or device, will be the source of entitlement identity.

Cancellation

Subscribers will be able to cancel from the payment provider's customer portal or the documented support route. Cancellation will stop the next renewal and will not remove existing local workspaces. The final checkout terms will state whether access continues through the paid period and how account recovery works.

Refunds

The final paid terms will state the applicable refund window, statutory consumer rights, and the process for requesting a refund. Refund, chargeback, and payment-failure events will revoke or change entitlement only after the payment provider confirms the event. Until a paid checkout launches, there is no ROVER purchase to refund.

Trial and promotional access

The current local trial lasts 15 days and starts only after an explicit action in the extension. Promotional, pilot, support, or gifted access may be issued separately in a future release with a stated duration and eligibility. Promotional access will not be sold or treated as a substitute for a confirmed purchase.

Support and changes

Use the support guide for product questions. A paid release will publish its final commercial terms, privacy disclosures, and store listing before launch. Material changes will be recorded in the change history.

Private by design

Your workspace stays yours.

The current beta stores workspace data in extension-local storage in your browser profile. It does not include an account, telemetry, or cloud upload.

What is stored

Workspace names, descriptions, URLs, tab titles, groups, collections, preferences, and local history.

What is not copied

ROVER does not copy cookies, passwords, sign-ins, form contents, private browsing sessions, or browser history.

Your controls

Export a backup, delete workspaces, or uninstall the extension. Review exported URLs and titles before sharing them.

Plans in development

Start local. Add more when you need it.

The beta includes a manually started 15-day trial. No payment or automatic subscription starts when it ends. Prices, checkout, and account activation are not live.

Free

Two saved workspaces, local capture, editing, restore, and import/export. If you already have more than two, your data stays viewable and exportable after trial expiry. You can rename and delete it; Free limits new workspace creation.

Pro Local

Planned subscription for unlimited workspaces, Custom Templates with placeholders, workspace combining, organization rules, version history, and scheduled capture. These local capabilities can be tested during the beta trial.

Pro Sync

Planned subscription adding optional account-based portability and encrypted backups through Google Drive, OneDrive, or ROVER. Profile separation and explicit sync choices remain central. Live provider connections and key recovery are not ready.

Your license follows you

The intended monthly or annual subscription belongs to your ROVER account rather than one browser or device. Home, Work, and other ROVER profiles remain separate; you choose which installation links to each. Complimentary Pro grants are planned for pilots and support.

Billing, refunds, cancellation, and final availability must be verified before paid launch. This page describes the intended offering, not an active purchase offer.
